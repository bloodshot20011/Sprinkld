Our Provenance & Heritage

Preserving the Living Heritage of Indian Botanical Terroirs

For centuries, industrial spice commodity supply chains have blended lots from dozens of farms, stripped volatile essential oils, and prioritized shelf life over aromatic potency. Sprinkled exists to restore the pure single-estate standard.

handshake

3.5× Fairer Farmgate

Direct contracts with master heritage growers across Kashmir, Kerala, and Tamil Nadu.

Pillar 01 · Radical Directness

Bypassing the Commodity Auctions

By funding regenerative soil practices and paying our partner farming families months in advance of the harvest, we secure the first canopy pick of every crop — unblended, untampered, and brimming with natural piperine, crocin, and curcumin.

Pillar 02 · Botanical Integrity

Cold Stone Milling & Micro-Batches

High-speed steel industrial pulverizers generate temperatures exceeding 80°C, destroying delicate floral aromatics before spices ever reach a kitchen. Sprinkled stone-mills all whole spices slowly under 32°C in small 15kg micro-batches.

science

< 32°C Micro-Milled

Zero thermal degradation of volatile terpene and aromatic phenol compounds.

Pillar 03 · Pure Vegetarian Dedication

Sanctified Purity Standards

Every botanical batch is handled exclusively in dedicated vegetarian processing facilities, sealed in recyclably shielded glass jars, and tested for heavy metals, pesticides, and artificial coloring agents.
