## Brand & Style
The brand conveys regal heritage, culinary provenance, and understated epicurean luxury. It caters to discerning gourmands, culinary professionals, and aesthetic-driven home chefs who view cooking as sensory art.

The visual style combines **Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Heritage**:
- Dramatic editorial framing inspired by culinary monographs and art publications.
- Generous, intentional negative space allowing rich product photography and deep spice hues to breathe.
- Hairline structural lines reflecting architectural precision and classical preservation.
- Quiet, cinematic motion: deliberate crossfades, subtle macro zoom on botanical textures, and graceful typographic reveals.

## Colors
The palette evokes the terroir of South Asian spice origins—rich soil, sun-cured saffron, polished mortar and pestle, and botanical deep reds.

- **Primary (`#4A121A` / `#5C1D24`):** Deep crushed burgundy and wild sumac. Reserved for dominant brand moments, high-impact titles, and prominent CTA anchors.
- **Secondary (`#D9822B` / `#C26D20`):** Sun-dried saffron and golden turmeric. Applied as a refined accent for badges, harvest indicators, tasting notes, and active hover states.
- **Background & Canvas:** Warm ivory (`#FAF7F2`) serves as the core canvas, layered with muted stone cream (`#F4EFEB`) for card modules and inset sections.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Warm stone hairline tone (`#E8E1D5`) provides structural boundaries without visual heaviness.
- **Neutral / Copy (`#1C1A18` / `#2A2624`):** Charcoal soot. Delivers high contrast for editorial readability while remaining softer than harsh pure black.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy strikes a balance between classical literary heritage and modern utilitarian clarity.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Employs expressive serif serifs, generous ascenders, and high editorial contrast. Display titles celebrate evocative vocabulary and poetic provenance.
- **Body & Microcopy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Anchors functional UI elements, tasting profile matrices, origin coordinates, pricing, and button labels with clean, rhythmic precision.
- **All-Caps Micro-Labels:** Rendered in small caps with wide tracking (`0.14em`) for harvest years, single-origin botanical regions, and section indices.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout employs an intentional, asymmetrical 12-column grid system paired with strict horizontal rhythm:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12 columns on desktop with generous 40px gutters; 6 columns on tablet; 4 columns on mobile.
- **Editorial Alternation:** High-density technical data modules (tasting curves, harvest analytics) alternate with wide, full-bleed cinematic imagery framed by hairline cream borders.
- **Section Transitions:** Extensive vertical clearance (`space-xl` and above) establishes a deliberate, unhurried pace suitable for a luxury house.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth avoids simulated heavy drop shadows, relying instead on material surfaces and crisp tectonic separation:

- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Structural cards, panels, and image frames are delineated by 1px borders in `#E8E1D5`.
- **Surface Layering:** Content elevation relies on alternating between the `#FAF7F2` canvas and `#F4EFEB` container surfaces.
- **Ambient Focus Elevation:** When popovers, flyout spice drawers, or quick-cart drawers surface, they introduce an ultra-soft, diffused amber-tinted shadow: `0 20px 48px -12px rgba(28, 26, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Glass Insets:** Sticky navigation bars and floating curation widgets utilize slight backdrop translucency (`rgba(250, 247, 242, 0.88)` with `12px` backdrop blur).

## Shapes
To reinforce an architectural, gallery-grade sensibility, this design system adheres to sharp, unrounded geometries (`0px` border radius throughout).

- Rectilinear cards, input fields, badges, and CTAs honor traditional apothecary drawers and classic bespoke packaging.
- Circular elements are strictly reserved for functional round icons (e.g., origin badges, flavor wheel indicators, and audio play controls).

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary CTA:** Solid Burgundy (`#4A121A`) fill, pure ivory text, zero radius, subtle padding (`1rem 2rem`). Hover state triggers a shift to rich wine (`#5C1D24`) with an expanded letter-spacing transition.
- **Secondary CTA:** Transparent background, 1px `#4A121A` border, charcoal text. Hover fills the container with `#FAF7F2` against a saffron accent border.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Small-caps microcopy accompanied by a trailing hairline arrow (`→`), underlined with a subtle offset border.

### Product & Story Cards
- Sharp, flat rectangles bounded by `#E8E1D5` hairlines.
- Imagery is framed in a 4:5 vertical editorial ratio with dramatic spotlighting on spice textures.
- Hover states initiate a quiet zoom on the botanical shot alongside an opacity reveal of origin notes (e.g., "Wayanad, Kerala • Elevation 900m").

### Chips & Badges
- Unrounded hairline tags with `#1C1A18` text on transparent or `#F4EFEB` surfaces.
- Saffron badge indicators (`#D9822B`) highlight single-estate and limited harvest lots.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Flat input surfaces encased by a single bottom border or full 1px `#E8E1D5` outline.
- Labels sit cleanly above the field in uppercase tracked sans-serif; active focus transitions the border to `#4A121A` without glowing ring offsets.

### Specialized Brand Modules
- **Tasting Wheel & Potency Scales:** Minimalist thin lines indicating heat, pungency, sweetness, and volatile oil content.
- **Origin Certificate Block:** Grid-aligned archival stamp section validating harvest date, lot number, and farmer collective attribution.
