The Culinary Philosophy

Architecture of the Scented Dum

Biryani is an exercise in restraint and geological stratification. In this classical Awadhi-inspired vegetarian preparation, hand-crafted malai paneer cubes and heirloom garden vegetables act as aromatic reservoirs, marinated gently in cultured milk, stone-ground ginger-garlic, and cold-pressed mustard oil before encountering the intense warmth of aged stone-ground spices.

Unlike heavy restaurant variants masked by artificial colourings and thick pastes, our formulation celebrates the crystalline purity of Himalayan Dehradun grains. Parboiled to exactly seventy percent elongation with crushed cardamom husks and quills of Sri Lankan cinnamon, each grain sits elevated, breathing steam through golden curtains of Pampore zafraan.

Sequential Technique

How To Make It

timer Active Kitchen Time: 45 Minutes • Slow Steam: 20 Minutes
01 15 Min Prep • 30 Min Rest

Marinade & Botanical Infusion

In an unreactive heavy earthenware or glass bowl, thoroughly blend the hung curd, freshly crushed ginger and garlic, cold-pressed mustard oil, pink salt, Salem turmeric, Kashmiri red chilli powder, and 2 tablespoons of Vaanika Royal Biryani Masala.

Gently fold in the fresh malai paneer cubes, par-boiled baby potatoes, cauliflower florets, and tender green peas, ensuring each piece is luxuriously cloaked in the spice marinade. Cover and let rest at cool room temperature for 30 to 45 minutes to allow the aromatic botanical oleoresins to infuse deeply.

02 & 03 Parboiling Precision

Grain Hydration & Whole Spice Parboiling

Step 02: Rinse the 2-year-old Dehradun basmati rice gently in cold running water three times until the runoff is clear of loose surface starch. Soak in cool mountain water for precisely 30 minutes, no longer, to avoid grain fractures.

Step 03: Bring 3 litres of fresh water to a rolling boil in a wide copper pan. Add rock salt (the water should taste seasoned like clean sea broth), whole green cardamom pods, cinnamon quills, cloves, shahi jeera, and tej patta. Tip the drained rice into the bubbling water. Cook over high flame for exactly 6 to 7 minutes until the grain has lengthened gracefully and snaps into three pieces when pressed—seventy percent cooked. Drain immediately through a wide perforated colander and spread over a parchment tray to halt residual cooking.

04 6 Minutes

The Ghee Searing & Subz Korma Base

In your primary heavy brass handi, warm 2 tablespoons of desi ghee. Introduce the marinated vegetables and paneer in a gentle layer over medium-high heat. Sear for 3 to 4 minutes until the yogurt and spices caramelize into a rich, fragrant korma base.

Delicate Heat Searing
05 5 Minutes

Architectural Dum Stratification

Evenly distribute the spiced paneer, vegetables, and rich korma gravy across the bottom of the handi. Layer fifty percent of the warm parboiled basmati rice over the base. Scatter half of the golden birista onions, freshly hand-torn mint, coriander, and a dusting of biryani masala. Crown with remaining basmati.

Layering: 2 Tier Alternation
06 2 Minutes

Saffron Infusion & Ghee Glaze

Drizzle the steeped golden Pampore saffron milk in deliberate concentric spirals across the white surface of the rice. Drizzle melted A2 cow ghee over the rice edges to cascade down the pot perimeter, finishing with 3 delicate drops of wild screwpine (kewra) essence and the rest of the golden birista.

Aroma Sealant Layer
07 20 Minutes

Sealed Hermetic Dum Cooking

Roll the soft wholewheat dough into a long rope and press firmly along the rim of the brass pot. Press the heavy lid downward to create an absolute airtight seal. Place the pot on high flame for 3 minutes until internal steam builds, then transfer immediately atop a heavy cast iron tawa (griddle) over lowest simmer for 18 minutes.

Indirect Radiant Heat
08 10 Min Rest • Unveiling

The Flavour Rest & Master Reveal

Extinguish the flame and let the sealed handi rest undisturbed for 10 full minutes. This rest is essential—it allows the trapped botanical vapour pressure to equilibrate and re-hydrate every individual rice grain without breaking. Slice through the crisp dough seal with a warm knife at the dining table. Use a wide, flat brass perforated ladle (kafgeer) to gently lift from the deep base, presenting the marbled amber and ivory grains onto warm bone china.

Technical Mastery

Chef's Notes & Guild Secrets

Small adjustments in moisture kinetics separate pedestrian rice preparations from regal Awadhi dum perfection.

hourglass_bottom

1. The 70% Grain Rule

If basmati is cooked beyond 70% in water, it will absorb the vegetable korma pan gravy too quickly and dissolve into mash under steam pressure. Test by snapping: it must split cleanly into three parts with a firm chalky core.

Structural Integrity
local_fire_department

2. Indirect Tawa Buffer

Direct gas flame against modern cookware creates localized scorching at the bottom layer. Always position a seasoned 5mm cast iron tawa between the open burner and your handi during the low-heat 18-minute dum cycle.

Heat Distribution
spa

3. Bruise, Never Crush

When adding fresh mint leaves to the dum layers, hand-tear or gently bruise them. Cutting with a dull steel blade oxidizes chlorophyll, imparting a bitter, vegetal astringency rather than crisp sweet menthol top notes.

Enzyme Care
cloud_sync

4. Rest Before Lifting

Never cut the dough seal immediately after killing the flame. The internal steam vacuum will collapse fragile starch lattices. The mandatory 10-minute resting period allows basmati starches to firm and temper.

Thermodynamics
Adaptations

Make It Your Own

Our spice architecture is calibrated to accommodate varied proteins and dietary standards without compromising aromatic balance.

Protein Shift

Soya Chaap & Lotus Stem

Substitute malai paneer with 400g grilled soya chaap chunks and 200g sliced Kashmiri nadru (lotus stem) for dense, fibrous absorption of the Awadhi spice bouquet.

Alternative: Smoked Tofu
Grain Adaptation

Brown Basmati Option

For whole-grain dietary regimes, use Himalayan Brown Basmati. Increase soaking to 2 hours and boil for 18 minutes to reach the 70% threshold. Requires 5 additional minutes of dum steaming.

Glycemic Index: Reduced by 35%
Plant-Based Fat

Strict Vegan Adaptation

Replace cow ghee with cold-pressed coconut oil or cold-pressed mustard oil. Substitute hung dairy curd in the marinade with cashew-cream curd blended with 1 tsp fresh lemon juice.

Zero Dairy Oleoresins
Herb Modulation

Sun-Dried Spearmint

In cold seasons when fresh mint lacks oil potency, rehydrate 2 tbsp of sun-dried Kashmiri spearmint in warm rose water for 10 minutes, using it sparingly between rice strata.

Heightened Menthol Concentration

Preservation Protocol

Care, Storage & Reheating

ac_unit

Airtight Refrigeration (Up to 3 Days)

Allow biryani to cool completely at room temperature for no longer than 60 minutes. Transfer gently to an airtight glass container to prevent volatile spice terpenes from diffusing throughout your refrigeration unit.

waves

The Skillet Steam Revival (Recommended)

Avoid microwave ovens which rupture grain cell walls into rubbery fragments. Instead, spread cold biryani in a wide pan, flick 2 tablespoons of warm water or saffron milk across the top, seal tightly with foil and a lid, and warm over low flame for 7 minutes.

inventory_2

Cryo-Freezing Notes

You may freeze vacuum-sealed portions for up to 1 month. Thaw overnight in refrigerator chambers before applying the skillet steam revival method.

Because vegetables release moisture during dum steaming, ensure you reduce your korma base slightly before assembling the layers.

30 to 45 minutes at cool room temperature is optimal for vegetarian ingredients. Paneer absorbs spices quickly without needing prolonged breakdown, while the cultured hung curd infuses the fragrant terpenes of our Awadhi Biryani Masala throughout the vegetables.
