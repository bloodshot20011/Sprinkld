Knowledge Archives

Common Questions

Immediate answers to queries regarding climate transit, shelf-life, and laboratory certifications.

All Vaanika orders leave our New Delhi archival cellar through our temperature-monitored BlueDart ClimateAir courier network. Once sealed in our nitrogen-flushed amber flacons, you receive an automated SMS and email dispatch bulletin carrying your telemetry lookup code. You may also enter your order identifier into the lookup module above at any moment.
Standard climate-safe surface shipping takes 2–4 business days across major hubs (Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad). For temperature-sensitive botanical lots such as fresh-ground turmeric and saffron stigmata, cold-chain air express guarantees delivery in 1–2 business days.
Yes, amendments to recipient addresses, customized gift inscription cards, or item lot additions can be executed within 4 hours of order placement. Once the nitrogen-sealing protocol commences in our vault, the manifest is locked to preserve volatile oil integrity. Contact the WhatsApp concierge directly for priority assistance.
We offer an absolute 100% replacement guarantee. Should your package arrive with damaged pharmaceutical glass, broken wax seals, or if the volatile aromatic potency does not match our gas-chromatography analysis sheet, our concierge desk dispatches a fresh single-estate allotment immediately without requiring returns.
Retain spices within their provided amber glass jars with the silicone-rimmed walnut wooden caps tightly sealed. Store away from direct sunlight, stove-side steam, or damp measuring spoons. Whole spices retain peak volatile oils for 18–24 months; single-estate stone-ground spices are best enjoyed within 9 months of grind date.
