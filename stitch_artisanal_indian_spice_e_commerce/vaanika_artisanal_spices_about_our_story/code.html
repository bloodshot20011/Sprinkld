The House Ethos

WE BELIEVE FLAVOUR MATTERS.

Spices are more than ingredients. They carry memories, traditions, micro-climates, and generational stories from one hearth to another.

For generations along the Malabar Coast and through the Kashmir Himalayas, the true essence of Indian spices was protected through sacred harvest calendars and deliberate granite pulverization. Before modern industrial processing traded volatile essential oils for prolonged supermarket shelf-life, spices were living, breathing botanical treasures.

Where It Began

FROM A LOVE OF AUTHENTIC FLAVOUR.

The idea began with a simple inquiry: why should extraordinary, freshly gathered spices be inaccessible to the home culinarian? In 2021, our founders set out along the cardamom hills of Idukki and the saffron basins of Pampore.

What we discovered was a vast disparity. Exceptional harvest lots were routinely aggregated, blended with inferior grades, and irradiated for export, stripped of their vibrant essence. Vaanika was established as an antidote: a direct estate conduit bringing single-origin, uncompromising botanical lots directly to the world's most discerning kitchens.
