About This Spice

"Prized for its luminous scarlet lacquer and gentle, warming sweetness without the searing burn of commercial hybrids."

Kashmiri Red Chilli Powder is prized for its vibrant natural colour and balanced, nuanced warmth without aggressive pungency. Carefully hand-harvested in the autumn mist of Kashmir and slow stone-pulverized below 35°C, it preserves delicate aromatics, carotenoids, and volatile oils that define authentic Indian culinary artistry.

Unlike industrial chilli powders that rely on Sudan dyes or heat-generating metallic pulverizers that cook out volatile flavour compounds, our Pampore lot is treated as an aromatic botanical. It yields an astonishing ASTA color score above 140, saturating curries, roasts, and gravies in brilliant amber-red while keeping capsaicin at a mellow 2,000 Scoville Heat Units.

How To Use It

Unleash the full potential of natural carotenoids and essential oils through temperature-disciplined culinary techniques.

Method 01 • Simmer

Dum Aloo & Curries

Bloom in hot ghee with hing and ground fennel. Imparts a radiant red lacquer to golden baby potatoes, paneer, roasted cauliflower, or braised lentils without scorched or acidic notes.

Method 02 • Marinade

Tandoori & Marinades

Whisk with thick hung curd, cold-pressed mustard oil, and crushed garlic. Delivers that signature deep crimson hue naturally without artificial food dyes.

Method 03 • Temper

Dal Tadka & Tempered Oils

Add to sizzling ghee or sesame oil right off the heat before pouring over yellow lentils or stewed greens. Instantly releases sweet paprika aromatics.

Method 04 • Finishing

Dry Rubs & Crisp Roasts

Dust over roasted sweet potatoes, crispy lotus stem, or spiced almonds. Provides an electric scarlet crust with mellow, warm earthiness.

The Heritage of Kashmiri Degi Mirch

Grown in the alluvial soils of Jammu and Kashmir where crisp mountain air and sharp diurnal temperature variations stimulate high concentration of carotenoid pigments (capsanthin and capsorubin). Unlike standard commercial ground red pepper, authentic Kashmiri degi chilli is famed for its non-irritating, gentle Scoville rating, rendering it suitable for children, sensitive diners, and opulent feast dishes that demand luminous coloration without punishing piquancy.

Preserving Volatile Carotenoids

Natural red carotenoids are sensitive to prolonged UV rays and ambient humidity. Store in this airtight amber glass jar away from direct stove-top steam. Always use a dry wooden or brass spoon when measuring.
